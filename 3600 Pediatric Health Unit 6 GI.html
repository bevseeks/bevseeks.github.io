General Pediatric GI Considerations

  • Infants' stomachs empty in 2–3 hrs → require frequent feeds
  • Liver & pancreas immature until ~6 months; no solids before 4 months
  • Pancreatic lipase inadequate until age 1 → can't fully absorb fats (e.g., cow's milk)
  • Birth weight should double by 6 months, triple by 1 year
  • Infants strain with stooling from immature muscle coordination — not always constipation
  • Breastfed infants: watery/seedy stools; formula-fed: soft/"seedy" stools
  • Toddlers: decreased appetite & metabolic rate vs. infants; food "jags" are normal
  • Preschoolers fluctuate overeating/refusal — never force feeding
  • Assessment order: LEAST → MOST invasive (inspect/auscultate before palpate)
  • Children are ticklish — palpate through clothing or lead with the stethoscope bell
