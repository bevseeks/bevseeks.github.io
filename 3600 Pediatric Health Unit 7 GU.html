AGN vs. nephrotic syndrome

The single most-tested distinction in this unit — anchor on blood pressure and albumin.
FeatureAcute glomerulonephritisNephrotic syndrome
TriggerPost-strep, symptoms weeks laterIdiopathic — minimal change disease
UrineGross hematuria — tea / cola coloredMassive proteinuria; hematuria usually microscopic
Blood pressureHypertensiveUsually normal or low
AlbuminRoughly normalLow — drives the edema
CholesterolNormalHigh
Edema mechanismFluid retention from ↓ filtrationFluid shift from ↓ oncotic pressure

Enuresis medication safety

DDAVP — first-line, works immediately. Watch dry mouth, headache, nausea.

Tofranil (imipramine) — second-line ONLY. TCA risk: cardiac arrhythmia, hypotension, hepatotoxicity.

Hypospadias / epispadias safety

No circumcision before repair — foreskin may be needed for reconstruction.

No tub baths until stent / catheter is removed.

Oxybutynin → constipation risk → prevent with fluids / stool softener.

AKI electrolyte chain

↓ H⁺/bicarb secretion → metabolic acidosis → worse hyperkalemia → Kussmaul respirations compensate → ↓ phosphate excretion → hyperphosphatemia → hypocalcemia → tremors / seizures.
